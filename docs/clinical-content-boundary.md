# Clinical-content boundary

The app is an educational demonstration, not a clinical product. Its lesson content is source-derived and intended to illustrate content modeling, bilingual parity, interaction design, and safety review.

The repository does not claim clinical approval, diagnostic accuracy, professional translation, patient outcomes, or production deployment. Any real-world release would require qualified clinical review, translation review, accessibility review with representative users, security review, and a defined incident and escalation process.

The optional AI companion is intentionally narrower than a general medical assistant. It is grounded with bounded lesson context, blocks or redirects diagnosis and treatment requests, screens likely identifiers, uses explicit consent, and falls back when the Worker is unavailable. It must not be used for urgent symptoms or treatment decisions.
