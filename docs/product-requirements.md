# Product Requirements

- Learners can navigate concise synthetic educational screens without an account or network.
- English and Spanish versions retain matching stable module and screen identifiers.
- A single-choice knowledge check produces a deterministic demonstration result.
- Progress can be serialized and restored by a local adapter.
- A narration cue can be passed to a platform speech adapter.
- Optional teach-back support is consent-gated, bounded, and failure-safe.
- Missing consent or service failure preserves access to the bundled lesson.
- Every screen is marked synthetic and carries a locale-specific not-clinical-guidance notice.
- The interface states that it is a portfolio demonstration with no organizational affiliation.
